# Example review profile

This is an illustrative public profile, not the owner's private preferences or history. Supply your own file through `RADAR_PROFILE_FILE` and keep it outside Git.

Look for concrete part-time projects in infrastructure, networks and business automation. Prefer an identifiable task, evidence of need, useful next action and explicit unknowns. Do not invent a budget, company size, availability or purchasing intent.

Distinguish an actual customer request from a vendor announcement, a potential partnership and a market reference. Professional contacts and events can be useful without a published contract.

For every candidate retain the exact source text and quote, explain the proposed fit, and list what needs confirmation. One rejected item does not automatically exclude its whole topic. Existing approvals must remain intact.

This profile is consumed by assistant review. Deterministic legacy selectors and route guards have their own explicit preset rules; changing this document does not silently rewrite them. Its hash is included in new review manifests.
