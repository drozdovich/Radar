# Telephony example tracks

The original product uses two independent commercial tracks alongside personal projects. Internal keys are stable for compatibility:

| Key | Question |
|---|---|
| `infinity` | Is there evidence of a party buying telephone service or call traffic? |
| `avans` | Is there a concrete inbound/outbound call task that voice automation could address? |

The preset distinguishes customer, partner and market reference. Selling a voice product does not prove demand to buy one. Email outreach, audio transcription and Russian-language text alone do not establish a phone-call use case or its market.

No companies, contacts or private customer decisions are distributed here. The public demo uses invented repair-shop and studio requests. Track policies are part of a concrete original preset, not a universal sales classifier.
